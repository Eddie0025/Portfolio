Meet Adityavir 👋

Hi there! I'm Adityavir, a Product Manager building AI/ML products and complex systems.

I work at the intersection of product strategy, technology, and execution—turning ambiguous problems into clear product direction, requirements, roadmaps, and measurable outcomes. As the Founder and Product Lead at Blackwood Technologies, I've worked across multiple AI product initiatives, from sovereign multi-agent cybersecurity and custom AI models to AI governance and decision-support systems. My role spans the journey from problem discovery and product definition to prioritization, execution, evaluation, and iteration.

With a background in computer engineering, I’m comfortable working closely with technical teams while keeping the focus on the bigger product question: what should we build, why should we build it, and how do we know it’s working?
